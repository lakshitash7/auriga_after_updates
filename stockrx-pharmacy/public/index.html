Never dispense an expired batch again.

StockRx is a batch-level inventory manager built for neighbourhood pharmacies. It tracks every batch's expiry, dispenses oldest-stock-first automatically, and warns you before anything goes out of date.

Get Started →

What it is

A full-stack pharmacy stock manager: register medicines, log batches with expiry dates, and dispense against real demand. StockRx always pulls from the batch expiring soonest (FEFO — first-expiry-first-out) and refuses to dispense anything already expired.

Key features

FEFO Dispensing

Automatically consumes the soonest-expiring in-date batch first, across as many batches as needed.

In-date Stock Lookup

Instantly see sellable stock per medicine, ignoring anything expired or quarantined.

Daily Expiry Job

Simulated-clock driven job flags batches expiring within 7 days and quarantines expired stock automatically.

Messy Data Import

Bulk-import batch lists with inconsistent formats — nulls, "10 units", mixed date formats, duplicate rows — and get a clean import report.

Re-order Alerts

When in-date stock for a medicine drops to or below its reorder level, a notification is logged to the outbox automatically.

Search, Sort & Pagination

Find medicines and batches fast, sorted by expiry, quantity, or name.

Who it's for

Independent pharmacies and small pharmacy chains that currently track batches on paper or spreadsheets, and need a lightweight system to reduce wastage and stockouts without enterprise software overhead.

How it helps

  • Cuts financial loss from expired, unsold stock via proactive quarantine + flagging.
  • Reduces manual FEFO tracking errors at the counter.
  • Prevents stockouts with automatic re-order notifications.
  • Turns messy paper/Excel batch records into clean, queryable data.
